# Keyboard benchmarks

Datasets for the typing engines (autocorrect, swipe, hit targets). `tools/build-benchmarks.py` builds them from sources that the bundled lexicon is not built from; see `data/LICENSES.md`.

- **Tune on `dev`, report on `test`.** The `*.test.*` files and `swipe-words.*` are frozen: their SHA-256 hashes are in the table at the end of this file, and the builder refuses to overwrite one that would change.
- `sentences.<lang>.<split>.txt`: one sentence per line, from the treebank's own dev and test splits, without sentences the treebank marks as holding a typo. Used for over-correction (type every token, count the correct ones that change) and for hit targets (simulated taps).
- `misspellings.<lang>.<split>.tsv`: `misspelling<TAB>correction`, real misspellings with exactly one listed correction. Split by a hash of the misspelling. The German list is short (64 test pairs): its figure moves by 1.6 points per word.
- `typos.<lang>-<layout>.<split>.tsv`: `up to three words before<TAB>typo<TAB>intended word`. One slip per word of three letters or more, drawn from the sentence split of the same name: a neighbouring key (60%), two letters swapped (15%), a letter missed (12%), a neighbouring key hit as well (13%). All-capitals tokens are left out.
- `swipe-words.<lang>.tsv`: `word<TAB>count`, the 10,000 most common words of two keys or more in the OpenSubtitles list. The German list is lower-case, so swipe results are compared without regard to case. Swipe paths and taps are generated by the Swift harness from fixed seeds (one seed for dev, another for test).
- `swipe-traces.real.en.jsonl`: 20 swipes recorded on an iPhone 16 Pro with a trace recorder that was in the keyboard during the phone test (since removed), one prompted word each: the word meant, the key centres and pitch at the time, and the finger path as `[x, y, seconds]`. Replayed by `sh scripts/test-keyboard-typing.sh swipe-real en`. It is a small tuning set, not a frozen test split: the simulated benchmark says whether the decoder works, this says whether it works on fingers.
